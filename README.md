# Production Performance & Operational Efficiency Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes six months of manufacturing data to evaluate production performance, quality, labor efficiency, and downtime across multiple production lines.

The objective was to identify performance gaps, investigate the main operational issues, and provide data-driven recommendations for improving production efficiency and operational performance.

## Business Problem

The manufacturing company wanted to understand why production performance was below plan and identify the main operational factors affecting productivity.

Management needed a clear view of production achievement, scrap rate, labor efficiency, and downtime across production lines.

The analysis aimed to identify underperforming areas, investigate the causes of performance decline, and provide actionable recommendations for operational improvement.

## Dataset

The dataset represents six months of manufacturing operations and includes:

- Production data
- Downtime data
- Quality data
- Inventory data
- Product information
- Production line information
- Machine information
- Material information
- Bill of Materials (BOM)
- Monthly production planning data

## Key Findings

- Line L02 recorded the lowest production achievement among the three production lines.
- L02 experienced a significant performance decline in Months 4 and 5.
- Machine breakdown and raw material shortage were the main downtime contributors.
- Machine M04 was the major contributor to machine breakdown downtime on L02.

## Root Cause Analysis

The analysis identified a significant increase in downtime on Line L02 during Months 4 and 5.

Machine breakdown and raw material shortage were the main downtime categories during this period. Machine M04 was a major contributor to machine breakdown downtime on L02 and was therefore identified as a key area for further maintenance investigation.

## Management Recommendations

1. Prioritize maintenance investigation for Machine M04.
2. Develop a preventive maintenance plan for Line L02.
3. Investigate the causes of raw material shortages during Months 4 and 5.
4. Improve material planning and replenishment monitoring.
5. Monitor Line L02 weekly using Achievement, Scrap Rate, and Downtime KPIs.
